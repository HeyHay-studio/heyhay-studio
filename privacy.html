Last updated: October 30, 2026

HeyHay Studio ("we", "our", or "us"), located in Accra, Ghana, respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our website visitors and users of our WhatsApp AI solutions.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website or use our WhatsApp integrations, we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Phone number, email address (e.g. studioheyhay@gmail.com), and name when provided voluntarily.
  • Chat Messages: Messages sent via the WhatsApp Business API to facilitate automated customer service replies and customer hand-off requests.

We use your information strictly for legitimate business purposes:

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors

We never sell, rent, or lease your personal data. We only process data through secure, industry-standard third-party providers:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Cloud API): Used to send and receive WhatsApp messages securely.
  • OpenAI: Used to generate AI responses according to business-provided instructions.

4. Data Retention & Security

We implement robust technical and organizational security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. Conversations and message IDs are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill service operations and debugging.
